Case study: transformer terminal clearance on a commercial solar project

Problem. Pad-mounted transformers supplied for several project sites had too little clearance between the neutral (X0) and Line A (X1) terminals to fit the required UL-listed lugs and conductors. That raised NEC compliance, safety and equipment suitability concerns, and it had to be fixed without more delays or risk to the warranty.

What I did. I led procurement coordination and supplier engagement. I gathered measurements, site photos and technical details from engineering, field contractors and suppliers. I evaluated alternatives such as UL-listed neutral extension lugs, approached other manufacturers, and coordinated a formal technical request to the transformer manufacturer on terminal spacing and approved termination methods. Throughout, I weighed cost, warranty responsibility and technical approvals.

Outcome. A potential workaround was identified for engineering and manufacturer validation, and the team gained an alternative sourcing route. It also showed the value of reviewing equipment specifications early.

Customer Experience Index (CXI) Prediction Model

MSc coursework · Middlesex University Dubai · 2026

A marketplace team wants to flag products likely to disappoint customers early, so it can drop them or plan marketing to improve them. I built a product-level KPI from Amazon review data, then trained models to predict it from product attributes alone, with no review data as input.

CXI = 0.5 × (mean rating ÷ 5) + 0.3 × (mean normalised sentiment) + 0.2 × (share of reviews with ≥ 1 helpful vote)

  • 6,327 reviews
  • 622 products with 5+ reviews
  • 80/20 train/test split
  • 12 input features incl. TF-IDF text
