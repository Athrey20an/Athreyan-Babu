Case study: transformer terminal clearance on a solar project

What I did. Led procurement coordination and supplier engagement. Gathered measurements and site photos, evaluated UL-listed neutral extension lugs, approached other manufacturers, and coordinated a formal technical request to the transformer manufacturer, weighing cost, warranty and approvals.

Outcome. A potential workaround was identified for engineering and manufacturer validation, and the team gained an alternative sourcing route.
